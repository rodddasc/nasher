Nasher Privacy Policy

Nasher is a personal tool used only by its owner. It connects to the owner's TikTok and Google Drive accounts through their official OAuth login. Access tokens and account names are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party. Videos are read from Google Drive (read-only) and uploaded to TikTok only when the owner chooses to publish. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok and Google account settings.
